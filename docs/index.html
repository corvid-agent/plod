What the words mean
  • ON TIME — the upkeep is funded and not yet due. A keeper is expected.
  • LATE — it is past its round and still unserviced. Any keeper may take it now.
  • GROUNDED — nothing is scheduled, or the escrow has fallen below one execution fee, so no keeper can be paid to run it.

These are Arcron's own three states — scheduled, due and dormant — in flight-board words. Dormancy is judged against the escalated fee, because that is what a keeper would actually be owed: an upkeep can starve at a balance its creator counted as several runs.

Read-only. Every number above comes from public TestNet state served by testnet-api.algonode.cloud — the keeper's upkeep boxes and Plod's own global state. Rounds are not a clock: Arcron promises "not before this round", never a wall-clock time, so every duration here is an estimate at ~2.695 s/round. Keeper 769891898 is live and not frozen.
